What is Malaysia?

Malaysia is a multicultural country with Malay, Chinese, and Indian populations. Be mindful of local customs, especially in rural areas. For example, dress modestly when visiting religious sites, and always remove shoes before entering mosques or temples. Malaysia offers a unique blend of modern cities, pristine natural landscapes, and rich cultural heritage waiting to be explored.
